Forty years of holidays on Om El Seed's shore.

Monte-Carlo opened in 1985 on the cliffs between Naama Bay and the Old Market. The reef starts at the end of our jetty, the aqua park sits beside the lazy river, and the Royal wing keeps a quieter, adults-only beach of its own.
